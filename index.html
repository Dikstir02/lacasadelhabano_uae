OUR STORY

THE HOME OF HABANOS IN THE UAE.

La Casa del Habano UAE is the Emirates' address for the authentic Cuban cigar — a house where Havana's unhurried ritual meets the warmth of Emirati hospitality.

Born from a love of Habanos and the culture around them, our Casas in City Walk, JBR and Abu Dhabi Mall are more than lounges and humidors. They are living rooms for connoisseurs and the curious alike: places to slow down, share stories, and savour every draw.

Every cigar we keep is 100% Cuban, sourced only through official Habanos channels and guarded in perfect condition — so what reaches your hand is exactly as the torcedor in Cuba intended.
